# S47 source and field audit

[Iranian Churn](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/563/iranian+churn+dataset), UCI Machine Learning Repository, donated April 8, 2020, [doi:10.24432/C5JW3Z](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5JW3Z), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The official ZIP is 41,943 bytes, SHA-256 `696c3a1812267980751f30d19193a1b430ac4ad76172bb089e664c96813ead66`; its Customer Churn.csv is 131,728 bytes, SHA-256 `90d5fb6bd1630cd4de4b4d28fcf8b4cb92a8f6ab7484605b0799d47386f7dbe1`. Source attribution does not imply endorsement. Raw rows and individual predictions are not published.

Actual file: 3,150 rows, 14 columns, no missing cells, 495 churn labels (15.714%). Despite a dictionary reference to anonymous ID, no customer ID is present. Nor are customer-level dates, repeated monthly observations, company identity or collection year available. The publisher describes features as first-nine-month aggregates and the target as status at month twelve. That supports a documented three-month planning gap, but exact field timestamps cannot be independently checked. Row order is never interpreted as chronology.

Normalize whitespace in column names only. The primary inputs are Call Failure, Complains, Subscription Length, Charge Amount, Seconds of Use, Frequency of use, Frequency of SMS, Distinct Called Numbers, Age Group and Tariff Plan. Complains is a binary flag, not a complaint count. No recency or usage trend can be reconstructed from one aggregate. Tariff is 1 pay-as-you-go / 2 contractual. Seven rows have Charge Amount 10 although the dictionary says 0–9; retain and flag this discrepancy. Age is exactly determined by Age Group and has only five values (15, 25, 30, 45, 55); do not treat it as actual continuous age or independent information.

Status (1 active, 2 non-active) and calculated Customer Value are excluded from the primary design because their operational definitions and calculation are insufficiently documented. They are not proven leakage; the publisher's timing statement covers them, but their apparent power requires an explicit sensitivity. Customer Value is neither a verified currency quantity nor measured profit. Include each/both only in clearly labeled derived-field comparisons.

There are 300 exact complete-row repetitions and 314 repeated all-predictor rows. The ten primary features form 2,826 distinct profiles, with 324 repeated rows; 22 profile groups have contradictory labels, totaling 83 rows. Largest group size is 12. Without IDs, it is impossible to establish whether repetitions are duplicated records or different similar customers. Retain rows, keep all identical primary-feature profiles together in every partition and cross-validation fold, resample whole profiles for uncertainty, and report equal-profile-weight sensitivity. Never claim that the 3,150 rows or 2,826 profiles are verified unique independent people.

Source descriptive counts: 241 complaint flags (200 churn / 41 non-churn); 245 contractual tariffs (6 churn / 239 non-churn); 154 zero-seconds-of-use rows. Non-active status has 370 churn / 412 non-churn; active has 125 / 2,243. These are associations, not estimates of a service intervention. Sparse contractual outcomes limit segment inference.
