# M5 source and information contract

The official [M5-methods repository](https://github.com/Mcompetitions/M5-methods) links the [organizer's dataset folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1wxz-TAfVE7uKGCjh405eCb2Q_pG3kAm9). This study uses sales_train_evaluation.csv, sales_test_evaluation.csv, calendar.csv and sell_prices.csv from that distribution. Exact public file URLs, byte sizes and SHA-256 values are in config.json. Download them through the publisher's normal controls into the external S02 cache, then run the verified ingestion. Large files may require Drive's size confirmation. No account restriction is bypassed.

The research uses the distributed competition data for a bounded, attributed methodological evaluation. No raw data redistribution license is asserted or granted. The source files remain outside Git and the website. Consult the publisher's current conditions before reuse. References: Makridakis, Spiliotis & Assimakopoulos, *M5 accuracy competition: Results, findings, and conclusions* (International Journal of Forecasting, 2022), and the official M5 Competitors Guide in the source repository.

The official release has five key fields (item, department, category, store, state), not the additional Kaggle ID convenience column. The calendar is consecutive daily rows beginning January 29, 2011 and lacks the convenience d field. Derive day indices only after verifying continuity and endpoints. Sales are nonnegative unit counts; prices are weekly dollars per item/store. Price rows must be unique by item/store/week. The study uses only prices from already completed weeks at every feature cutoff. It does not assume next week's actual price was known.

The full source covers 3,049 products across ten stores, three states, three categories and seven departments. The prespecified cohort retains three deterministically hashed product IDs per department, with prior observed prices in every store: 21 items / 210 product-store series. It does not represent a random sample of all US retail demand. Exact selection is in config.json.

The target is each next 28-day sales total. No inventory availability, latent demand, retailer costs or realized profit is provided. Economic outputs replay observed daily sales under explicitly assumed stock and cost rules. The metrics are cycle-total forecasts, not an official M5 leaderboard score.
